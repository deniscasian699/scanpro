## 🔧 Common Issues & Solutions

### 📷 Camera or Gallery Scanning

- Allow Camera permission for live scanning. Gallery import remains available without it.
- Improve focus, lighting and contrast; keep the entire code and its quiet zone visible.
- Use the explicit MSI/Pharmacode mode where required, and keep Pharmacode upright.
- For ZIP import, select an archive containing supported code images.

### 🏷️ Barcode or QR Validation Error

- Check the selected format, required digit count and check digit.
- Code 39 requires its supported uppercase character set; Code 128 has ASCII/length limits.
- Link QR requires a valid website address; use Text QR for plain text.
- PDFs and files need a public HTTPS URL. ScanPro does not upload or host documents.

### 🎨 Custom Code Does Not Scan

- Increase contrast, reduce the logo and use standard modules if necessary.
- Keep quiet zones intact and test the exported PNG/SVG/PDF on the intended scanner.
- Retail presets do not issue GS1 identifiers or guarantee retailer acceptance.

### 🗂️ Free History and Daily Limits

- Free keeps the newest 50 entries total across all categories; new entries replace older ones.
- Free allows 5 scans, 5 QR creations and 5 barcode creations daily, plus 1 batch export and 1 batch scan session.
- Where available, an optional rewarded ad grants one extra use after the reward is earned.
- If an ad is unavailable, try later; ads cannot be guaranteed.

### 💎 Premium Not Activating

1. Ensure you are signed in to Google Play with the account used for purchase.
2. Try: **ScanPro Settings → Restore Purchases**
3. Wait a few minutes and restart the app.
4. If still not working, contact us with your **Google Play Order ID.**

### ❤️ Monthly Subscription Still Active After Buying Lifetime

Google Play does not allow apps to cancel subscriptions on your behalf.
You must cancel the monthly subscription manually:

→ [play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions)

Your Lifetime access is already active. Cancelling the subscription
simply stops future monthly charges.

### 🔔 How to Remove Ads

Purchase either the **Supporter** (one-time) or **Monthly Supporter**
(subscription) tier. After purchase, all advertising is removed permanently
for Supporter, or for the duration of your subscription for Monthly Supporter.

**Note:** Premium and Premium Lifetime do **not** remove advertising.
They unlock unlimited daily scans, QR/barcode creation and bulk uses,
history and advanced code customization. Advertising may return after
ad-removal access expires or is refunded/revoked, subject to privacy choices.

1. Go to [play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions)
2. Find your ScanPro purchase.
3. Request a refund through Google Play according to its current refund policy.

[support@rdcapps.com](mailto:support@rdcapps.com) with your
Google Play Order ID and we will do our best to help.
