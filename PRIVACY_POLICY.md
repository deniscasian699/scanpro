## 2. What the App does

ScanPro lets you scan QR codes and barcodes with the camera or from gallery images, generate QR codes (link, text, Wi-Fi, contact, email, payment/crypto requests and public PDF/file links), create 1D barcodes, use batch generation/reading and ZIP exports, keep a local history, and optionally purchase Premium or Supporter. PDF/file QR codes contain a public link; ScanPro does not upload or host your files or process payments.

## 3. Data we process

### 3.1 Data stored only on your device

- **Scan & create history** — content you scanned or generated, timestamps, filters and saved code appearance. Free keeps the newest 50 entries in total across all categories. Stored in app private storage (SharedPreferences / local files). Not uploaded by us.
- **Settings** — theme, vibrate/sound, auto-copy, purchase status, daily free quotas, QR/barcode appearance, optional logos, consent choices and ad scheduling counters.
- **Camera frames** — processed on-device for barcode detection; not recorded or sent to our servers.
- **Gallery images** — decoded locally when you pick an image to scan or a logo; not uploaded by us.

### 3.2 Data we do not collect ourselves

**Local code processing:** ScanPro displays advertising through Google AdMob, subject to applicable privacy choices. Advertising services may process the data described in section 4. We do not operate a backend that stores your QR contents, contacts, or Wi-Fi passwords, and we do not require an account for basic use. 

### 3.3 Permissions

- **Camera** — live scanning;
- **Internet / network state** — used by Google Play Billing and RevenueCat for optional purchases, and Google AdMob/UMP for advertising and privacy choices;
- **Vibrate** — optional haptic feedback on scan;
- **Photos / media (via system picker)** — optional scan-from-image and Premium logo.

## 4. Advertising and privacy choices

ScanPro uses **Google AdMob** for banner, native, interstitial, app-open and optional rewarded ads. A rewarded ad may grant one extra use after a daily limit is reached, when an ad is available and the reward is earned. Watching is optional. Ad availability is not guaranteed.

When advertising is enabled, Google may receive IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), advertising/app identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic data for delivering and measuring ads and preventing abuse. Data handling depends on consent, device settings and Google's services. ScanPro does not send QR contents, Wi-Fi passwords, contact fields, camera frames or selected images to AdMob as ad-targeting data.

Google's **User Messaging Platform (UMP)** manages applicable privacy choices. Ads are requested only when the consent framework permits requests. Where a consent form is required, it is presented before advertising. You can revisit available choices through **Settings → Ad privacy choices**. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized, non-personalized or limited; non-personalized advertising does not necessarily mean that no data is processed. Android also provides advertising-ID controls.

**Premium and Premium Lifetime include ads.** Only **One-time Support** or an active **Monthly Supporter** entitlement removes advertising. AdMob is not initialized while verified ad-removal access is active, and ad slots are hidden. If that entitlement expires or is refunded/revoked, advertising may resume subject to applicable privacy choices. Previously processed data remains subject to the provider's retention rules.

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information for advertising](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
- [Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure)

## 5. Purchases and RevenueCat

Optional Premium purchases and subscriptions are processed by **Google Play Billing** through **RevenueCat**. Google handles payment details. We receive only the purchase and entitlement status needed to unlock the purchased features — unlimited scans, QR/barcode creation, daily bulk uses and history, custom QR/barcode styles and logos, or ad removal for Supporter products. Premium and Premium Lifetime do not remove ads. RevenueCat processes this data under its own privacy policy as a service provider.

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)

## 7. Your rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent.

- Clear scan history in the App (Settings / History);
- Uninstall the App to remove all local data;
- Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions;
- Revisit available advertising choices in Settings → Ad privacy choices and use Android advertising-ID controls.

For privacy requests, contact us via the links in section 1.

## 8. Children

## 9. Data retention

- Advertising, consent and diagnostic data processed by Google is retained according to Google's applicable policies. Removing ads does not automatically delete previously processed provider data.
