# Changelog

Changes to ScanPro are documented here using the structure of
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/).

---

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Google AdMob banner, native, interstitial, app-open and optional rewarded ads
- Google UMP privacy choices and Settings consent entry point
- Dedicated barcode creator and industry presets
- Payment/crypto, email and public PDF/file-link QR types
- Android Share integration for incoming links and text
- QR gradients, shapes, corner styling, frame presets, logos and visual color picker
- Independent appearance samples and generated-style History snapshots
- PNG, SVG and PDF export, batch ZIP generation and ZIP image reading
- Guides and FAQ describing current features, Free limits and paid products

### Changed
- Premium and Lifetime unlock features but include ads
- Only One-time Support and Monthly Supporter remove advertising
- Free History keeps the newest 50 entries total across all categories
- Unified creator controls and wider tablet layouts
- Portrait on phones; rotation available on tablets
- Interstitials are navigation-only, with persistent counts and startup protection
- Clear history dialogs share the app theme in Settings and History
